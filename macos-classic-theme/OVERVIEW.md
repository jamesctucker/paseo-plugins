# macOS Themes

macOS Themes gives Paseo the chrome of a native Mac app in four palettes, picked in Settings, under Appearance.

macOS Classic light and dark follow the palette of the macOS Classic theme for Zed by huacnlee, and sit comfortably next to other apps styled the same way.

macOS Tahoe light and dark are lighter and cooler, with the near-invisible separators and faintly tinted sidebar of current macOS. Panels read as layered panes rather than boxed regions.

A theme sets color only. Glass effects such as a blurred desktop showing through the window belong to the app's desktop window, not to a theme.
